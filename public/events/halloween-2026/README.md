# Halloween 2026 archived audio

Original event recordings, played on demand by the keepsake cards:

- `witch-laugh.mp3`: “Witch's Laugh.wav” by NikoMyth, [Freesound 348142](https://freesound.org/people/NikoMyth/sounds/348142/), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `train-whistle.mp3`: “Train whistle, foley #6” by Joseph SARDIN & Axeline T., [BigSoundBank 3320](https://bigsoundbank.com/train-whistle-foley-6-s3320.html), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Both recordings are unedited, with reduced playback volume.
- `bat-wings.wav`: “Bat wings” by Gammelsmurfen778, [Freesound 546823](https://freesound.org/people/Gammelsmurfen778/sounds/546823/), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Derived from the MP3 preview: 2.3–3.9 seconds, normalized to 65% peak, with a 25ms fade-in and 60ms fade-out; mono 44.1kHz WAV.
- `puca-ghost.wav`: original synthesized audio without third-party samples. Originally bound to `PUCA_MARKER_SVG` for stale vehicles, not to the witch-hat or sleeping Púca illustrations. Retained as an archived recording; neither Púca keepsake card has a sound control.
- The pumpkin bus horn is synthesized in the app.
